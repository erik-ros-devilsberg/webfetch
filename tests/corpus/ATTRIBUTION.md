These PDFs are synthetic, not web snapshots: redistributable PDFs with
clean, known text are hard to source offline, and the gate's job is to
catch regressions in our own extraction (text, metadata, normalization).
Regenerate with `MinimalPdf::withText(...)` — the prose and Title/Author
are inlined in the generator call that produced them.
